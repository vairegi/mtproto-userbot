WHAT WENT WRONG IN v12.56 (from your screenshots):
  1. Section rendered INSIDE the sheet body → always appears above the
     actions row (Download / Save / Share). sheet.js layout is:
        [handle] [title] [body] [actions]
     so anything you append to body is above actions by design.
  2. Heading text was invisible on your theme (dim var(--du-ink-lo)).
  3. `scrollSnapType: proximity` still locked scroll on Android WebView
     inside a bottom sheet — snap of any kind is the "stuck" culprit.

FIXES IN v12.57:
  a) DOM placement: mountSimilarRow now receives sheet.el (the sheet
     ROOT), not the body. It appends the section AFTER the actions bar,
     so "Similar to this" sits BELOW Download/Save/Share as requested.
  b) Header: uppercase 14px 800-weight in var(--du-ink) (bright white on
     dark), plus a small "swipe →" hint on the right. Impossible to miss.
  c) Scroll: scrollSnapType removed entirely (was the sole cause of the
     "frozen" feel). Free horizontal scroll with iOS momentum, isolated
     touch region (contain: content), right-edge fade gradient as the
     peek affordance.
  d) Re-mount guard: if paintFull runs twice (cache paint + fresh paint),
     the second call now removes the previous section before rebuilding
     — no more duplicate rows.
