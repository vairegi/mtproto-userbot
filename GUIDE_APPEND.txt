WHAT CHANGED
Bot2Fetcher/app/meta.py:
  1. _clean_title() now removes EVERY [..], (..), {..} segment anywhere in
     the caption title — artist circles like [Kuroiwa Menou], nested forms
     like [Aokihoshi (Zen Shiro)], parody suffixes like (Goddess of Victory:
     Nikke), language/scanner tails like [English] {Zombii}. Innermost-first
     loop handles nesting; leftover double-spaces and dangling separators
     (" -", " |" at the ends) are cleaned up. If stripping would leave an
     empty title, the original is kept (safety net).
  2. The Tags row now starts with ➲ instead of ➤. All other rows
     (Groups/Parodies/Artists/Characters/Languages/Categories) keep ➤.

EXAMPLE (unit-tested):
  Before: [Kuroiwa Menou] Hitozuma to Shounen Hirusagari no Yuuwaku |
          Married Woman and Boy: Early Afternoon Temptation
          (Hitozuma Club Glass no Kutsu) [English] {Zombii}
  After:  Hitozuma to Shounen Hirusagari no Yuuwaku |
          Married Woman and Boy: Early Afternoon Temptation

SCOPE: NEW cover posts only. The 13,096 covers already in Main/BackupDB
keep their current captions (Telegram captions can be edited later if you
ever want a cleanup pass — separate task).
