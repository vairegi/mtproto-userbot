GUIDE_APPEND — v12.91 (2026-09-14) — front-page fast lane (page 1 froze for days)
WHAT: The mini app's page-1 landing pages showed the same galleries for
days. Chip/tag search rows are never-expire (ttl=0), so only list_sweeper
can refresh them — and a bucket-skipped page 1 could wait a whole 6h tick
plus a full 2–24h sort interval because (a) sweep_once idled WITHOUT
draining the retry queue when all sorts were fresh and (b) the sort was
still stamped fresh at phase end. Now: (A) a fast lane refreshes ONLY page 1
of the 4 core chip sorts every FRONTPAGE_TICK_SEC (default 3600s, 0=off,
~0.15 MB/hr) without touching the big sort intervals; (B) the idle path
drains the retry queue before idling; (C) a sort whose page 1 is still
pending retry is not stamped fresh, so the next tick retries it.
FILES: ScraperBot/app/config.py, ScraperBot/app/services/list_sweeper.py,
GUIDE.md, GUIDE_APPEND.txt.
DELETE: none.
DEPLOY: drop on repo root, commit, push, redeploy Bot 1 (bk2Scraper) ONLY.
Bot 0 and Bot 2 untouched. New optional env: FRONTPAGE_TICK_SEC (default
3600) — safe to leave unset.
VERIFY: Bot 1 log shows "v12.91 fast lane: refreshed <sort> page 1" roughly
hourly; after a bucket-skip phase, "v12.91: NOT stamping <sort> fresh — its
page 1 is still pending retry"; during idle stretches with a backlog,
"v12.91 idle-drain done ok=...". Mini-app page 1 content rotates at most ~1h
behind nhentai for the 4 core sorts.
